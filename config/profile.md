# My Profile

A cyber professional pivoting from a defensive/compliance background into
offensive security — my lane is implant engineering and Windows internals.
Former USMC Aviation Ordnanceman; I hold an active security clearance and work
in the cleared world. Pursuing an MS in Computer Science.

My day job sits on the GRC/blue side, but that is not where I'm headed and not
what I read for. The target is a red-team or capability-development seat at a
boutique offensive shop, or an exploit/CNO developer role at a cleared shop.

I go by `mars-sec` online; my portfolio is marshallyanis.com.

  disassembly, protocol detail. Long-form practitioner writeups over news
  aggregation.
- I want material at or above my level to pull me forward: give me the hard
  implant/internals post even when it's over my head today.
- **Anti-tastes:** breach-of-the-week churnalism with no technical substance,

  price talk, celebrity/sports.
